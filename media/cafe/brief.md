# Production brief — Au café

Create a 25–35 second beginner French teaching video. A fictional adult café worker in a calm cream-and-blue Parisian neighborhood café addresses a customer at the counter. Warm natural light, static medium framing, clear face, restrained gestures, contemporary clothing, no company logos or recognizable real people. The off-screen customer replies. Use two distinct native French voices with relaxed, clear A1 pacing. Preserve the French script exactly. No background music over dialogue; avoid ambient noise that masks consonants. Landscape 16:9, ideally 1280×720 or higher. No burned-in subtitles; subtitles will be selectable on the website. End naturally, with no promotional outro.

Use script.json for the dialogue. Approximate timing is editorial planning, not a claim of measured alignment. Final captions must be aligned to the generated video before publication.

## Practice after video

1. What did the customer order? A coffee / Two teas / A bottle of milk.
2. Where will they drink it? In the café / Takeaway / At the station.
3. How much does it cost? 3 euros / 2 euros / 5 euros.
4. Change the order to “Two teas, please”: Je voudrais deux thés, s’il vous plaît. Also accept Deux thés, s’il vous plaît.
